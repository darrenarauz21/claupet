3. Your Claude account
Claupet reads what Claude Code already stores on your computer (credentials, sessions and usage) to show it to you. 4. Your data
Claupet stores your settings, your history and the downloaded models on your computer. It doesn't store passwords or copy your Claude Code conversations anywhere else. The project has no servers of its own: it doesn't receive your data. Claupet connects to the internet only for what you use, for example: your accounts' usage (Anthropic), your questions to Claude, the weather when you ask for it (Open-Meteo and, to find your city, Windows location or your IP address with ipwho.is), new versions (GitHub; can be turned off in Settings) and the models you turn on (Hugging Face and GitHub).

5. What it does on your computer
With your permission, Claupet adds hooks to the settings.json of each Claude Code folder (it saves a backup first), can start with your system and, on Windows, creates shortcuts. When you ask it to, it takes actions on your computer, such as screenshots, clipboard, windows or reminders; each kind of action asks for permission the first time. On Windows, uninstalling it from Settings › Apps removes the hooks, start with your system, the shortcuts and pending reminders.

6. AI answers
